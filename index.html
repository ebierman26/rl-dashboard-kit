RL Dashboard
For Rocket League on Windows · Free

Your stats, every match.

RL Dashboard records every Rocket League match you play. You get an overlay on top of the game (session record, streaks, MVPs, who you're playing) and a stats dashboard. Everything stays on your PC.

Installing

The blue box only has a "Don't run" button.
Click More info (the underlined words) first. Then Run anyway appears.
Windows Security says it found a threat (e.g. "Trojan:Win32/Wacatac").
That's a false alarm. Windows sometimes guesses that a new program from a small developer is a threat, especially an installer few people have downloaded yet. The file has been reported to Microsoft as safe, and you can check it yourself: 69 antivirus programs scanned this exact file (VirusTotal), and none of them flagged it. To install anyway:
  1. Open Windows Security › Virus & threat protection › Protection history.
  2. Click the RL-Dashboard-Setup.exe entry, then Actions › Allow on device (or Restore).
  3. Download it again if it was deleted, and run it.
Rather not? Download the zip version instead: right-click it › Extract All, then double-click START HERE.
It's stuck on "Setting up…".
The Yes/No permission box is probably hiding. Look for a flashing shield on the taskbar, click it, then click Yes.
I clicked No, or it says "Almost done".
Right-click the tray icon › Settings › Turn on, click Yes, then restart Rocket League.
It installed, but I can't find it.
Double-click RL Dashboard on your desktop, or click ^ by the clock to find the tray icon. Right-click it › Quick tour to see where everything is.
I already have it, or the installer says it's already installed.
You're all good: run the installer anyway. It updates the copy you have, in the same folder, and keeps your name, settings and match history.

Playing

The overlay doesn't show in game.
Set Rocket League to Borderless (step 6). Still nothing? Press Ctrl+Shift+O, or right-click the tray icon › Show overlay.
The overlay keeps saying "Rocket League is starting…".
The game's stats aren't on yet. Right-click the tray icon › Settings › Turn on, then restart Rocket League.
The overlay won't let me click it.
It's locked, so clicks go through to the game. Right-click the tray icon, then Unlock overlay.
A win or loss didn't count.
Right-click the overlay, then Fix record to add or remove one.
Will Rocket League think it's cheating?
No. It uses the game's official Stats API, which Psyonix provides for tools like this. It doesn't touch the game's files or memory, and the overlay is a separate window.
My friend's boost numbers for the same game are a little different from mine.
That's normal. Each PC measures boost from its own copy of the game's live data, about 30 updates a second, and the two PCs don't get their updates at exactly the same moments. When boost goes up and down within a split second (grabbing a pad while boosting), one PC can catch a bit more of that back-and-forth than the other, so boost used and picked up can differ by a percent or two. Both still agree on the tank from start to finish, and neither is wrong. A big gap (10% or more) would be worth reporting.
Why don't Dropshot games show shots?
In Dropshot, the game counts almost every hit that damages the other team's floor as a shot (40+ in one game is normal), so shots don't mean anything there. Dropshot games show your Damage and Ultra damage instead, and they're left out of your shot totals, average shots, shooting % and shot records. The game only sends damage for you, so other players show a dash.

Settings and more

Show RL Dashboard on my Discord profile?
Right-click the tray icon or the overlay › Show on my Discord profile (click it again to turn it off). The Discord app has to be open on the same PC (Discord in a web browser can't see it). Not showing up? In Discord: User Settings › Activity Privacy › turn on Share your detected activities with others.
Change my name or turn off start-at-login?
Right-click the tray icon, then Settings.
I play on more than one account.
Right-click the tray icon, then Settings. Type each account's name and press Enter to add it to the list, then click Save and start. Games on any of them count, and the dashboard gets an Account picker to see them together or one at a time.
Hide something on the overlay?
Right-click the overlay › Overlay look › Show, and untick anything you don't want: your all-time record in the mode, the session totals, the last-10 blocks, This game, teammates' stats, extra stats, Last game, players you've played before or the Did you know? facts. Tick it again to bring it back. To turn off pop-ups like the goal flash, use Overlay look › Flashes. The stream overlay has its own choices in 📺 Stream mode.
New PC? Bring my match history with me.
On the old PC: right-click the tray icon › Settings › Save a copy… (under "Your match history") and save it somewhere you can reach from the new PC, like a USB stick or OneDrive. On the new PC, install RL Dashboard, then Settings › Restore from a file… and pick that file. It adds the games the new PC doesn't have yet and keeps any it already has, so it's fine if you've played there already.
Show my stats on stream (OBS or Streamlabs)?
Open the dashboard and click 📺 Stream mode (next to Arrange). Pick a color and size, check the preview, then click Copy link. In OBS: Sources › + › Browser, paste the link into URL, and set the width and height it shows. It appears while Rocket League is running, with goal flashes, the result after each match and the private match cards, and the space around it is see-through.
How do the weekly challenges work?
Every Monday there are three new ones (easy, medium and hard) on the dashboard's Overview tab, with a progress bar each. Everyone with RL Dashboard gets the same three, so you can race your friends. Your games from Monday to Sunday count, on all your accounts; private matches don't. When a match finishes one, the overlay says ✅ Weekly challenge done after it.
Why does it look different around holidays?
The overlay and dashboard get a holiday look for Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's, Valentine's Day, St. Patrick's Day, the 4th of July and Halloween, then go back to normal. To turn it off, right-click the tray icon or the overlay › Settings, untick Holiday themes and click Save and start.
How do I uninstall it?
Right-click the tray icon › Settings › Uninstall… (or Windows Settings › Apps › RL Dashboard › Uninstall). It asks whether to keep your match history. If you pinned RL Dashboard to the taskbar, right-click that icon › Unpin from taskbar: Windows only lets you remove pins yourself.

Something else, or an idea? Send feedback or report a problem (it needs a free GitHub account), or right-click the tray icon or the overlay › Send feedback or report a problem, which fills in your version for you. What you write there is public, so leave out anything private.

Day to day just play

Just open Rocket League. Everything is already running.

Overlay

Drag it where you like. Right-click it for everything: its look (size, compact mode, lock), Settings, Fix record, updates and Quit. Ctrl+Shift+O shows or hides it, even in game.

Dashboard

Opens when the game starts, or click the RL Dashboard icon. The pickers at the top (mode, competitive or casual, time, account and car) filter every tab. Click the arrow on a box's title to fold it.

Tray icon

The RL icon by the clock (under the ^ arrow on Windows 11). Click it for the dashboard; right-click for everything else.

Updates

A notification pops up by the clock (or a green Update ready bar on the dashboard and overlay): click it, read what's new, click Install. Your stats and settings are kept, and nothing installs without your OK.
